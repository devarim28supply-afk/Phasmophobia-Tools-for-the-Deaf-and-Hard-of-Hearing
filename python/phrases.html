PhasmoSound phrases

In the game: F5 opens the board, press the number to say a line, F5 again for the next page of 9, Esc closes.

Order matters: 1 to 9 is page one, 10 to 18 is page two, and so on. Put the lines you say most at the top. Lines without audio are spoken by Kokoro the first time (about a second), then cached.

ElevenLabs audio (optional, better voice, free tier is enough for this list)

Only lines without ElevenLabs audio are generated, so re-running costs nothing for existing lines. Edited lines get new audio next time you generate.
